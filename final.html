    🤖 What is the AI Price system?

    AI calculates an exact price based on your website type and selected features. The price stays locked — it's revealed LIVE only after your ₹650 one-time unlock fee is approved. This fee is fully adjusted in your final price.

    💳 Where does the ₹650 fee go?

    The fee is never wasted! Once your price is unlocked, if you order the website, the ₹650 is adjusted from your final price. Finding out your price is completely risk-free.

    ⏳ How long does approval take?

    Usually 10-30 minutes. The moment the owner approves your payment, your price appears on your screen automatically — no refresh needed.

    📅 How many days does the website take?

    Most websites are ready in 2-4 days. Larger projects (online stores, admin panels) may take 5-7 days.

    🛠️ Are changes free?

    Yes! Changes are completely free until you're satisfied. Small changes after delivery are also covered under support.

    🌐 What about hosting and domain?

    Hosting setup guidance is free. You'll need to purchase the domain yourself (only ₹200-400/year) — I'll help you through the entire process.

    📩 How do I pay?

    Pay the ₹650 AI Price unlock fee via the UPI QR code. The final website payment is confirmed on WhatsApp — half in advance, half on delivery.
